Ford builds the park one narrative at a time. We build ours the same way: eight builds across the semester, each one a question about consciousness, the self, and the masks that make it. This is our workshop.

01 / The roll

Eight builds, punched in order

Like the player piano in the Mariposa, our semester is a roll of punched notes. Each build is one note. Published narratives are ringed in gold; the blank holes are still being written.
